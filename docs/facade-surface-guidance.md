# Facade source and host ownership guidance

New source-bound tasks now receive a version-2 surface declaration packet.
Alongside interior finish sources it lists existing `facade`, `panelFacade`,
`edgeFacade` and `storeyFacade` sources, their exact declaration hashes,
separate host ownership and original layout/depth/exclusion fields. Arrays are
copied, not aliases of the accepted scene. This is declaration data, not a
certified cell map, new permission or automatic geometry edit.

A window can fit the host and approved region but still touch a protected
host-wall cell when its width, spacing or recess changes. Owning the window
source does not mean owning the whole host. New blueprint instructions ask
the designer to allocate the intended full facade language before freezing
package responsibilities. Role instructions explain this distinction before
the first attempt, not only after a rejected compiler/package result.

No compiler, cell-owner, route, geometry quota or placement check is relaxed.
The instructions preserve design freedom within actually allocated surfaces
and available air; they do not impose a stock facade or silently shrink a
proposal. Original frozen tasks/runtime/journals are not patched or resent.
Provider token inheritance and task call ceilings remain unchanged.

Authored rectangular and polygon probes reproduce a protected-wall aperture
growth rejection and show that legitimate owned refinements still pass.
Synthetic staged-dispatch tests check that both blueprint and role prompts
carry the guidance. These are mechanism evidence, not proof of higher real
architectural quality or acceptance of an installed binary.
