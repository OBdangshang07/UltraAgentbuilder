# Coupled player flow

The selection panel has a separate new-coupled reference-picture task and
history. It does not convert an older single-call or whole-assembly SEND into
new consent. The model, effort, pictures, annotations, prompt, tier and complete
shared budget must match the current explicit choice. Only the new independent
SEND button may dispatch; unknown outcomes continue observing the original.

The original complete candidate is downloaded and checked in the background
against the same retained Capture. The world projection remains at original
coordinates inside W minus P. BEFORE, AFTER, changes, layers and category
filters affect display only, not the complete application scope. A red deletion
marker does not remove a world block. New-building movable projections remain
separate and cannot accidentally use the fixed-patch confirmation path.

Application has an independent server audit, full fresh BEFORE, acknowledgement
of unverified physics/circulation and a one-use final confirmation. New matching
door pairs are complete operation groups; existing coupled/dynamic background
blocks remain protected. Closing or resizing an unconfirmed page invalidates
its preparation. Closing confirmed progress does not cancel its server task;
explicit cancel and current operation queries are separate controls.

Protected undo has another independent confirmation for the same retained live
sealed prefix. A changed door member preserves the complete pair and its
written support. Protection compares original AFTER states: edits subsequently
changed back to identical states have not been independently verified as a
history-sensitive case. Do not infer unconditional preservation of every edit
history or region-file durability. Important worlds require backups.

Coupled display/HUD lifetime is separate from legacy display. All world,
dimension, player, page and original candidate fences remain in force; a late
callback or cleared HUD cannot supply world authority. Renderer reload/close
and hotkey suppression include the coupled overlay; shader/physical input
compatibility still requires installed tests.

These source paths do not enable normal paid dispatch or replace process-owned
Bridge admission flags. Bridge capability implementation/availability flags and
ordinary startup gates must be reconciled after installed acceptance. Static
contracts and compilation are not a player-facing release, real image/model
quality proof, Iris/IME acceptance or physical-save acceptance.
